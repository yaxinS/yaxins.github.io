<h3>LeetCode-style platform for practicing Machine Learning and Data Science</h3>

Recently, I started to relearn Machine Learning in a more systematic way. During this process, I discovered Deep-ML, a platform for practicing Machine Learning and Data Science through hands-on problems.

We can choose problems based on difficulty level and topic. The platform covers a wide range of topics, including **Machine Learning**, **Deep Learning**, **NLP**, **Linear Regression**, and **Computer Vision**.

If your solution is incorrect or there are errors in your code, hints are also provided to guide you toward solving the problem.

If you are looking for a practical and structured way to strengthen your Machine Learning skills, I would definitely recommend checking out Deep-ML.
